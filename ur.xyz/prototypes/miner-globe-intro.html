Miner Globe Intro

A miner is a provider, an extender, or both. The intro opens on the two roles as the app draws them, a filled dot and a hollow ring, brings them together, then pulls back to a globe of miners and lets every country take its own color. It plays once; the colored globe keeps turning.

Timeline

0.0 – 0.5 s
Roles appear. A provider dot fades in on the left, an extender ring on the right, both in provider green, each with its label underneath.
0.5 – 1.9 s
Merge. They travel to the center; the two labels fade as they close in. In the last third of the move the dot contracts to the size a dot has inside a ring, and the ring closes around it.
1.9 – 2.8 s
Lock. The ring settles with a small pulse and "miner" appears underneath. This is one miner playing both roles, drawn exactly as the apps draw a provider reached through an extender.
2.8 – 5.0 s
Zoom out. The miner label fades as the camera pulls back about that miner. The rest of the world's miners fade in as they enter the frame: dots, rings, and dots inside rings, all green. The pull-back is eased on a log scale so the speed feels constant.
5.0 – 5.6 s
Full globe. A hold on the whole planet covered in green, centered on the merged pair.
5.6 s
Rotation begins. The globe eases into a west-to-east turn (one revolution in 48 s) and the view tilts down from 40° to 22° over four seconds.
6.2 – 8.6 s
Country colors. A wave spreads out from the merged pair across the surface. Each miner it reaches fades from green to its country's color in 0.6 s.
9.6 s
Intro ends. The colored globe keeps rotating for as long as the page is open. Nothing replays on scroll.

Parameters

  • Miners on the globe1,309 land dots · 3° spacing
  • Role mix (seeded)50% provider · 25% extender · 25% both
  • Geometry, per grid celldot 0.28 · ring 0.36 (stroke 0.09) · dot in ring 0.16
  • Colors#87FB67 provider green, then the SDK's country palette
  • Where the roles meetthe land dot nearest Madrid; the globe opens centered on it
  • Rotation7.5° per second · tilt 40° → 22°

Implementation notes

  • Canvas 2D, no library. The land dots come from Natural Earth 110m, sampled at 3° with the country of each dot resolved at build time: about 20 KB of data.
  • Country colors are the SDK's palette (93 countries with providers). A country outside it takes the SDK's mix of two palette colors, as the apps do.
  • Plays once per page load and never replays on scroll; a refresh starts it over. With reduced motion the page shows the final colored globe, not rotating.
  • Proposed placement: the Miners section's opening visual, above the intro paragraph, square, up to 640 px wide.
